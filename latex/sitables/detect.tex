\begin{flushleft}\footnotesize The scale factors move by at most 1.0\% and the paired RMSE stays between 8.2 and 13.1\,\wn{} across the 16 combinations, and by at most 0.8\% across the eight that use the criterion adopted here, so neither the detection threshold nor the tolerance drives the calibration. The penalized RMSE charges every unpaired band a fixed 35\,\wn{} so that the cells remain comparable. It is an imputation at the tolerance constant and not a bound: a band left unpaired because nothing lies within 35\,\wn{} has a true residual larger than 35, while one whose partner was claimed by the assignment may have a smaller one. Roughly nine tenths of the penalized mean is contributed by imputed terms, which is why it is stable near 26\,\wn{} across criteria; we report it beside the paired RMSE rather than in place of it.\end{flushleft}
