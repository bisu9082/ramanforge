# WARNING — the entry labeled HN2 is not HN2

The measured spectrum deposited under the label `HN2` is the spectrum of **liquid
benzene**, not of bis(2-chloroethyl)methylamine. Its four strong bands at 605.6, 993.6,
1176.3 and 1585.4 cm-1 are the Raman-active fundamentals of benzene, reproduced to within
1.7 cm-1 and in benzene's own intensity order, and the three positions a monosubstituted
ring would require (702, 787, 1030 cm-1) are all below 1.6 sigma of the local noise.
Section S5.2 of the manuscript's Supplementary Material gives the analysis.

HN2 dissolved in that benzene is neither confirmed nor excluded: two of the four bands
Kondo et al. (2018) report for HN2 are present at three standard deviations or better in
every acquisition, and a third in two of three. We therefore do not claim the vial is
mislabeled, only that what the instrument measured is not the neat agent.

**Do not use this entry as a reference spectrum for HN2 or for any nitrogen mustard.** It
is retained in the deposit because the manuscript's query set must be identical under both
libraries, and because the comparison that exposed it is itself a result of the paper.
